BRG(1)borgchart manualBRG(1)

NAME

brg - run teams of coding agents from plain files

SYNOPSIS

brg <COMMAND> [OPTIONS]

DESCRIPTION

Describe each role's model and standing task in a template folder, then brg create <template> <folder> starts a team from it, working in that folder. brg status shows who is alive, what they are doing and their unacked message counts; brg attach drops you into a member's live session; brg stop stops it all, and brg start brings it back.

Members are claude and codex sessions on a private tmux server. They talk over a message bus of durable plain messages and acknowledgements, and a team can be a member of another team.

Early, and expected to change.

QUICK START

$ cargo install --git https://github.com/mrshll/borgchart --locked --bin brg borgchart
$ brg validate ./lamp-eng
$ brg create ./lamp-eng ~/src/lighthouse
$ brg status -w
$ brg attach lamp-eng-x7k2/lead-q8m3
$ brg stop lamp-eng-x7k2

Needs Rust 1.89 or newer, tmux 3.2 or newer, and the claude and/or codex CLIs.

COMMANDS

Team templates

templates
List available team templates and their teams
validate
Check a team template and brg's configuration; starts nothing
secrets
List the secrets a template's plugins need, and whether each is set

Teams and members

create
Make a team from a team template, or a member of a role
start
Bring a team to what should be running, or start one member
stop
Stop a team and all its child teams deepest first, or one member
delete
Remove a stopped team, or a stopped member
status
Show a team tree, or every top-level one, with nested child teams
attach
Open a member's live session (detach with your tmux prefix, then d)
logs
Show a member's or plugin's recent output

Messages

msg
Send, list, read, acknowledge, wait for and check messages

Cleanup

cleanup
Remove what brg left behind for deleted members and child teams
destroy
Stop a whole tree and delete its records, notes and messages

brg help <command> or brg <command> --help gives a command's details.
