BRG(1)borgchart manualBRG(1)



NAME


brg - run teams of coding agents from plain files



SYNOPSIS


brg <COMMAND> [OPTIONS]



DESCRIPTION


Describe each role's model and standing task in a template folder, then brg create <template> <folder> starts a team from it, working in that folder. brg status shows who is alive, what they are doing and their unacked message counts; brg attach drops you into a member's live session; brg stop stops it all, and brg start brings it back.


Members are claude and codex sessions on a private tmux server. They talk over a message bus of durable plain messages and acknowledgements, and a team can be a member of another team.



COMMANDS



Team templates




templates
List available team templates and their teams


validate
Check a team template and brg's configuration; starts nothing


secrets
List the secrets a template's plugins need, and whether each is set




Teams and members




create
Make a team from a team template, or a member of a role


start
Bring a team to what should be running, or start one member


stop
Stop a team and all its child teams deepest first, or one member


delete
Remove a stopped team, or a stopped member


status
Show a team tree, or every top-level one, with nested child teams


attach
Open a member's live session (detach with your tmux prefix, then d)


logs
Show a member's or plugin's recent output




Messages




msg
Send, list, read, acknowledge, wait for and check messages




Cleanup




cleanup
Remove what brg left behind for deleted members and child teams


destroy
Stop a whole tree and delete its records, notes and messages




brg help <command> or brg <command> --help gives a command's details.



QUICK START


$ curl -fsSL https://borgchart.ai/install.sh | sh
$ cd path/to/your/repository
$ brg create engineering


You need tmux 3.2 or newer, git with a commit name and email set, and the claude and codex CLIs logged in. Keep ~/.local/bin on PATH.


This starts the engineering team in a git repository you want the team to work on: a Claude lead that starts Codex helpers as needed. Your plans must offer the models pinned in the role files. The included seating plugin checks subscription quota before helpers launch and waits if it cannot establish availability.


The installer configures the bundled templates and auto_trust: true when no configuration exists; it preserves an existing config and tells you if you need to add the templates folder. Set out your own instruction-file rules.


create prints the team's tree. Replace engineering-k3f9/lead-q8m3 below with your team and lead IDs. Wait for brg status to show the lead idle. If blocked at a prompt, use brg attach engineering-k3f9/lead-q8m3, answer it yourself, and detach with Ctrl-b, then d.


Send a first task:


$ brg msg send engineering-k3f9/lead-q8m3 "Write HELLO.md in this folder with one line saying hello, then reply to me by message. Do it yourself; start no helpers."


Read the reply, check the result, and acknowledge the message (replace m2 with its ID):


$ brg msg list --team engineering-k3f9 --full
$ cat HELLO.md
$ brg msg ack --team engineering-k3f9 m2


The reply appears when the lead finishes; run msg list again if it has not arrived. You can also talk to the lead through brg attach.


Stop when done:


$ brg stop engineering-k3f9


brg start engineering-k3f9 brings it back with its notes and messages. See installation for supported systems, paths, pinned versions and building from source.
